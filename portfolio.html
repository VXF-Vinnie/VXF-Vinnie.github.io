Split — iOS Bill Splitter

Swift · SwiftUI · Two-person team · In development

An iOS app that helps groups divide a restaurant bill quickly and accurately. Instead of one person totaling the check, Split assigns each item to the people who ordered it and calculates what everyone owes, including their proportional share of tax and tip.

  • Building with SwiftUI and an MVVM architecture, with SwiftData for bill history and MapKit / Core Location for nearby restaurant lookup.
  • Designed to support even or itemized splits, shared items, and 18%, 20%, 25%, or custom tips distributed proportionally.
  • Team workflow uses feature branches, pull requests reviewed by the other teammate, and GitHub Issues with a project board.
